A Distinctive Professional Foundation

Withbert W. Payne, CPA received his early education at an English boarding school in the Himalayas, before earning his General Certificate of Education from the University of Cambridge. He subsequently trained as a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales — the FCA designation — one of the most demanding and internationally respected professional accounting qualifications in the world, and the British equivalent of the American CPA.

That foundation — Cambridge, the ICAEW, and early experience in rigorous independent analysis — established the intellectual framework that defines his approach to every client engagement today.

"My background is not in selling insurance. It is in auditing, analyzing, and evaluating financial structures independently. I apply that same discipline to every long-term care engagement — no carrier bias, no promotional agenda."

Insurance Review Services

What We Do — and How We Do It

Insurance Review Services specializes in reviewing and optimizing long-term care policies for professional and high-net-worth individuals and couples. Our objective is straightforward: minimize cost, maximize benefit, and ensure that no premium dollar is wasted.

Our Objective

Minimize Cost. Maximize Benefit.

We identify LTC policies that minimize our clients' costs and maximize their long-term care benefits. We accomplish this through specialized product knowledge and long-standing relationships with leading LTC carriers — relationships that enable us to access pricing and structures that may not be visible through a single-carrier advisor.

In many cases, we identify coverage structures that provide a full return of all premiums paid, less any LTC benefits received. This means the premiums you pay are never simply lost — if care is never needed, the value comes back.

Our Approach

Analyze. Design. Recommend.

Every engagement begins with a thorough analysis of the client's actual LTC insurance needs — income, assets, health history, family situation, and existing coverage. We then design a responsible protection program and recommend an insurance strategy that is cost-effective, appropriate for the client's individual situation, and defensible from an independent analytical standpoint.

Mr. Payne personally participates in every LTC policy review. This is not a delegated or automated process.

Why Independent Review Matters

A Second Opinion Belongs in Every LTC Decision

To avoid a conflict of interest — and to obtain a genuinely objective perspective — your long-term care coverage should be evaluated by someone other than the agent who sold you the policy. The agent who sold you the policy has an inherent interest in defending it. An independent review has no such constraint.

Withbert W. Payne, CPA personally conducts every review — applying the same independent analytical framework he developed at Price Waterhouse and refined across decades of carrier-side and consulting experience.

No Fee
